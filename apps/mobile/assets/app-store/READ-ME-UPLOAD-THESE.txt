## Review Information → Screenshot (REQUIRED for IAP)
Use ONE of these (portrait phone size — NOT the square 1024):

1. **TRY FIRST:** `iap-review-1320x2868.png`   (1320 × 2868)
2. If ASC still errors: `iap-review-1284x2778.png` (1284 × 2778)
3. Or: `iap-review-1260x2736.png` (1260 × 2736)

Also same image as: `iphone-07-pro.png` (now 1320 × 2868)
